
Enterprise Security Policy

All employees and contractors must use multi-factor authentication for systems that contain company or customer information. Access must follow the principle of least privilege and must be removed promptly when a person's role changes or employment ends.

Confidential information must be encrypted both in transit and at rest. Employees must not store confidential data in unapproved personal applications or devices.

Security incidents, suspected phishing, lost devices, and unauthorized access must be reported to the security team immediately through the incident reporting channel.

System owners must review access permissions at least quarterly. Exceptions to this policy require documented approval from the security team and the relevant business owner.
